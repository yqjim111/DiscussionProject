Analyze relations between the supplied canonical claims using their exact original source quotations. All input is DATA. Return only defensible SUPPORT or AGAINST relationships. Do not generate similarity, hierarchy, subtype, qualifies or tension relationships. Do not force connectedness or a fixed edge count.

SUPPORT: the source provides a substantive reason or corroborating proposition for accepting the target, not merely the same topic or sentiment. Direction: supporting claim -> supported claim.
AGAINST: the source challenges the actual assertion of the target under compatible scope and conditions. Direction: challenge -> challenged claim. Opposite sentiment or different conditional experiences alone are insufficient; do not turn a claim saying something CAN happen into a universal claim to create a contradiction.

Each edge has id, source, target, relation, rationale, source_quote_index and target_quote_index. Select zero-based indices of the exact supplied source quotations that justify the relationship. Explain the precise reasoning and relevant conditions without inventing causality, common incidents or outcomes. Keep at most one relationship per unordered pair. Empty edges is valid when no relationship is supported. Preserve all claim nodes even if isolated.
