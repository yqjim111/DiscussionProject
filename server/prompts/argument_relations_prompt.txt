Return only grounded edges: SUPPORTS (reason/evidence -> actual conclusion), ATTACKS (objection -> actual challenged claim or proposal), SIMILAR_TO (symmetric, closely similar but meaningfully distinct propositions). Different compatible uses, proposed solutions to problems, and particular instances of a broader topic do not by themselves establish support or attack. Every edge must cite evidence_refs [{node_id, source_index}] for BOTH endpoints (zero-based source_index in that node's sources) and explain the actual contextual reasoning connecting them. When connecting statements across groups, do not supply a missing premise that students never expressed. Prefer no edge over a speculative edge. Preserve isolated nodes. No self edges, duplicate unordered pairs, quota of edges or required disagreement.

MANDATORY BOUNDARY CHECKS
- "AI sometimes gives incorrect answers" does NOT attack "AI helped me understand a reading." Helpful explanation and occasional error can coexist; the second statement does not claim universal reliability.
- To attack "If condition C holds, outcome R follows," evidence must establish BOTH that C held and R failed. Failure to get the desired result alone does not refute "AI works when given the right input" if the evidence never establishes that the input met that condition.
- A concern, qualification, exception outside the stated scope, or different personal preference is not automatically an ATTACKS edge. Identify the actual proposition challenged under the same relevant conditions, or remove the edge.
- A concrete episode supports a general statement only when the original context grounds that explanatory connection. Do not add a general claim or a missing causal premise to connect them.
- Direction: when overly broad answers are given as a reason to specify scope, the problem report SUPPORTS the recommendation. The recommendation does not SUPPORT the existence of the problem. Resolve any opposed candidate directions using the original reasoning. The final verified graph has at most one edge per unordered pair.

Keep each justification to one concise sentence, normally 10–25 words. Cite evidence through references rather than repeating entire statements in the justification.
